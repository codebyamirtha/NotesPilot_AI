🗣️ Spoken Punctuation Voice Cues

Speak any of these verbal cues while voice recording to insert instant formatting:

"period" / "full stop"

Inserts . and capitalizes next word

"comma"

Inserts , clause pause

"question mark"

Inserts ? inquiry mark

"exclamation mark"

Inserts ! emphasis mark

"colon"

Inserts : list delimiter

"semicolon"

Inserts ; linked clause

"new paragraph"

Inserts double line break \n\n

"open / close quote"

Inserts "..." quotation marks

⚡ Natural Pause Detection: Pausing speaking for > 1.2s automatically finishes the sentence with a period and prepares the next word for capitalization!
